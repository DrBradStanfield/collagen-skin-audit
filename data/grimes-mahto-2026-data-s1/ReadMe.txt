README.TXT
------------
The spreadsheets in this work contain the extracted data from collagen hydration and elasticity studies contained within the meta-analyses examined. Percentage conversions were performed using a delta-method, papers quoting SEM were converted to SD for consistency and cross-comparison.

The associated R files perform meta-regression on this data, and produce relevant figures. They are provided as is, and reproduce the figures given in the paper. For further information, please contact:

davidrobert.grimes@tcd.ie
